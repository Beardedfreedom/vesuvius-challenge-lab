This volume represents a vital primary/secondary narrative anchor regarding St. Augustine & Florida History. Written or compiled in **2001** by **Defense Technical Information Center**, the work captures critical contemporary observations, colonial documentation, institutional shifts, and geographic assessments of St. Augustine and the surrounding Floridian borderlands.

Key thematic pillars emerging from the full-text analysis include:
1. **Institutional & Military Architecture**: The central role of defensive fortifications, including the coquina-quarried Castillo de San Marcos and outer defensive lines.
